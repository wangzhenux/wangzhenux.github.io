Components · Forms

Form controls

Inputs, selects, toggles, and the specialized widgets that show up once a form grows up — searchable selects with quick-clear, multiselect with an invert action that flips the selection, status-aware helpers (including the often-forgotten warning state), a compact date picker, and a range picker with presets.

01 · Fields & status

Inputs · selects · textarea

Borders are faint so filled forms read as content, not chrome. Status tints the border + a 3px soft ring: focus, warning, error, success. data-state="warn" is distinct from error (red) — a warning is "check this" rather than "this is wrong".

01b · Affixes

Prefix & suffix — icon or text

Use .input-affix around an input or combobox trigger. Slot .affix-prefix or .affix-suffix with either .affix-icon (14px stroked SVG) or .affix-text (monospaced, muted). Use the inline --affix-prefix-w / --affix-suffix-w vars when the affix text is wider than the 32px default.
