<section id="s01">
  <Lede>
    ServiceNow is a platform-as-a-service (PaaS) provider of enterprise Service
    Management (SM) software. Onboarding new hires is one of its most frequently
    used functions — through ServiceNow, managers assign equipment, set
    permissions for new employees, and track the onboarding process.
  </Lede>

  <BodyText>
    The two main problems of the existing site were a **non-intuitive workflow**
    and **confusing taxonomy**. Over two coordinated, iterative research phases,
    I researched users, ran usability testing, and redesigned the new-hire
    onboarding experience.
  </BodyText>

<section id="s02">
  <SectionH>Research Study I</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    The ServiceNow New Hire Process redesign and research efforts were conducted
    in iterative, coordinated phases. During the first phase, generative research
    was conducted to determine key user needs. Following data collection,
    alternative designs were produced. The second phase of research was dedicated
    to evaluating those designs.
  </BodyText>

  <SectionH small>Phase 1 goals</SectionH>
  <CardRow>
    <PainCard num="01" title="Document needs and pain points">
      Document ServiceNow users' primary needs and key pain points, focusing on
      the new-hire onboarding process.
    </PainCard>
    <PainCard num="02" title="Recommend a redesign">
      Provide a set of recommendations for redesigning the onboarding elements of
      ServiceNow.
    </PainCard>
    <PainCard num="03" title="Prioritize future work">
      Provide insights into what other elements or processes addressed through
      ServiceNow could be improved in later redesign efforts, and how to
      prioritize those efforts.
    </PainCard>
  </CardRow>

  <SectionH small>Heuristic evaluation</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    Prior to user feedback sessions, a heuristic evaluation was completed. This
    enabled the researcher to better understand the ServiceNow product, evaluate
    it against standard usability heuristics, and identify potential pain points
    for users during testing. The major issues surfaced:
  </BodyText>

  <Dual>
    <DualCol heading="Major issues from heuristic evaluation">

      <div class="dual-item"><div class="head">Non-interactive info boxes</div><div class="det">Info boxes that you can't hover into or interact with, while holding lots of content.</div></div>

  <SectionH small>User feedback sessions II</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    **9 Rackspace managers** participated in second usability feedback sessions,
    lasting approximately one hour each, with high-fidelity prototypes.
    Participants varied by department/team (e.g. different types of support
    groups, sales, investment) as well as experience level. After each session,
    on a scale of 1 to 100, participants rated each prototype based on how well
    they expected it to support them in the new-hire process.
  </BodyText>

  <BodyText>
    **Results: the rating for the prototype increased from 65 to 85.** In terms
    of design preference, a slight majority (5 out of 9) preferred the single-page
    form; however, order effect may have been a contributing factor to
    participants' preferences — 6 out of 9 participants preferred the second
    design they were presented with.
  </BodyText>

  <SectionH small>Top findings &amp; design decisions II</SectionH>
  <Dual>
    <DualCol heading="Top findings">
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Labels still confusing to users</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Confused by language of progress-bar stages</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Concerns over logistics of desk location</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Business costs of hardware selections</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Desired to view permissions of mirrored Racker</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Unclear or missing fields in submission process</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Unsure how to create/edit autofill templates</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Separate sections of Comment Center confusing</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Desired status check of submitted ticket</div></div>
    </DualCol>
    <DualCol heading="Design decision">
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">New labels and tooltip help throughout the form</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Clearer wording to indicate ticket path and status</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Select general area instead of specific location</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Add informative icons and alert message</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Able to display all permissions of a Racker</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Scroll to and highlight the missing fields</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Created more intuitive UI with role-based templates</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Combined all comments into a single page</div></div>
      <div class="dual-item"><div class="det">Provided live status updates and a comment section</div></div>
    </DualCol>
  </Dual>
