<section id="s01" data-reveal>
  <Lede>
    UnitPulse set out to make property leasing an AI-native operation — to find
    renters, convert them, and measure the whole funnel without the manual glue
    landlords live with today.
  </Lede>
  <BodyText>
    To move fast and learn, we deliberately shipped three focused products
    independently: **UP Marketing** to generate demand, **UP Leasing** to convert
    it with AI, and **UP Insight** to measure everything. Shipping them separately
    let each move at its own speed and prove its own value. That independence was
    the right call to start — and it set up the harder, more interesting design
    problem that came next.

  <BodyText>
    So we converged the three products into a single AI-native leasing platform —

  <SectionH wide>How I made the convergence real</SectionH>
  <SectionDeck>Three pieces of work made the convergence real. Expand each for the detail.</SectionDeck>

  <ExpandModule
    n="1"
    title="The Copilot: one front door for the AI suite"
    role="Role: Product Design Lead — interaction prototype + generative-UI container"
    href="/work/crm-copilot"
    linkText="Read the full Copilot deep dive →"
  >
    {/* EXAMPLE VISUAL — replace with final Figma export */}
    <ImageShowcase>
      <ShowcaseImage
        src="/cases/unitpulse-platform/copilot.svg"
        alt="The Copilot: a CRM page with a chat sidebar — a typed command, a working-on-it state, and a generated result card"
        width={1600}
        height={835}
      />
    </ImageShowcase>
    <BodyText>
      **The problem.** Agents faced 15-click menu paths and pinged support just to
      ask whether an action was even possible; the AI bots (Listing, Leasing) had
      no unified command surface.
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **My design.** A conversational command center plus an adaptive
      **generative-UI container** that renders model-produced dashboards, forms,
      and tables on the fly, with context isolated per browser tab. (PRD §5.4,
      assigned to me.)
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **Principles.** Conversational-first; beat-the-menu perceived speed (an
      instant "working on it" state plus command shortcuts mask model latency);
      graceful failure; and progressive discoverability that surfaces relevant
      commands in context.
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **Targets (goals, not results).** The PRD set these as targets: 10+
      natural-language operations, a >80% command-success rate, and >75% weekly
      active usage. Copilot-specific usage was not independently instrumented, so
      these stay goals. The CRM served **~170 agents at peak** (Pendo).
    </BodyText>
  </ExpandModule>

  <ExpandModule
    n="2"
    title="The Portal & Design System: making the merge safe"
    role="Role: Product Design Lead — UX layer, DS primitives, feature surfaces"
  >
    {/* EXAMPLE VISUAL — replace with final Figma export */}
    <ImageShowcase>
      <ShowcaseImage
        src="/cases/unitpulse-platform/portal-merge.svg"
        alt="The merge: three separate login screens collapsing into one authenticated portal shell"
        width={1600}

    </ImageShowcase>
    <BodyText>
      **The merge.** Unify Insight, Marketing, and Messages into one authenticated
      SPA with namespaced routes — while the legacy apps keep deploying until a
      phased cutover, so there's no big-bang switchover.
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **What I owned, honestly.** I led the UX layer, canonicalized the design-
      system primitives, and owned several feature surfaces. The absorption
      architecture was a shared effort (the plan doc was written by another
      engineer). The design system is shared — I introduced and hardened specific
      primitives (a canonical DSConfirmModal; a unified filter-toolbar trigger) and
      the CLAUDE.md anti-pattern rulebook that killed recurring UI bugs.
      {/* ‹C-split› UX-layer vs merge-architecture credit; ‹C-ds› which primitives originated vs extended */}
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **Velocity, with AI in the loop.** 48 PRs merged in about 7 weeks, with
      Claude as a pair-author (co-author trailers on 53 commits) and CodeRabbit AI
      review in the loop that I triaged. The framing matters: AI accelerated
      execution; the judgment — what to build, what to canonicalize, what to defer,
      and what to keep honest — was mine.
    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>
      **Status.** Built and merged to main, **pre-cutover**: phases 0–3 done, 4
      partial, 5 pending. Not yet launched or in production.
    </BodyText>
  </ExpandModule>

    </BodyText>
    <BodyText>

      **A decision I'd feature.** I dropped "Qualified Prospects" because the term

      definition travels with the data it explains — designing around a
      data-accuracy constraint, since the predecessor dashboard had been hidden for
      being inaccurate.
