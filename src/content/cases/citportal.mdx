    The Combinatorial Interaction Testing (CIT) web portal serves as a resource
    for combinatorial interaction testing across many domains.
  </Lede>

  <BodyText>
    The original version was a static page with no database behind it. To make
    the content easy to maintain and update, I re-implemented it as a dynamic
    site. I worked closely with my sponsor to settle the requirements and
    determine the entities the site needed, drew the ER diagram, and built the
    database in MySQL and PhpMyAdmin.
  </BodyText>

<section id="s02">
  <SectionH>Building the site</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    I developed the site in PHP with CodeIgniter, using the Gumby framework to set
    up the grid and arrange content. As an informative site, I organized the
    content as pieces of information boards and used light but high-contrast
    colors — mostly black, white, and gray with a few highlight colors — to keep
    the content clear and well arranged.
  </BodyText>
