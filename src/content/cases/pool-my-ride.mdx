<section id="s01">
  <Lede>
    T.E.A.M. (Together Everyone Achieves More) Carpool is an iOS app built to help
    University of Texas at Austin students find a carpooler.
  </Lede>

  <BodyText>
    Based on UT's 2014 Parking Strategies Committee Report, the campus had about
    52,000 students, 17,279 faculty and staff, and 5,000 visitors against only
    15,800 parking spaces — a potential user-to-space ratio of 4.8:1. To ease the
    crunch, UT lets up to four members share a parking permit and carpool to
    campus. But few students knew the policy existed, and the hard part was simply
    finding a carpooler. T.E.A.M. set out to make that fast and easy while
    surfacing the permit policy.
  </BodyText>
</section>

<section id="s02">
  <SectionH>User research</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    We ran a survey and two rounds of interviews, plus a competitive analysis of
    Uber, Zimride, and Pool My Ride to learn from their strengths and weaknesses.
    The survey drew 43 responses across five topics — demographics, ways to find a
    carpooler, carpooler type, reward, and difficulty finding a carpooler. After
    the initial round we narrowed scope to UT students and folded UT's carpool
    policy benefits into the system: reserved carpool parking, reduced permit
    fees, and an extra UT shuttle pass.
  </BodyText>
