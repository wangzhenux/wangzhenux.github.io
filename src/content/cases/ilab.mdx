<section id="s01">
  <Lede>
    A platform for the innovation lab to better assign students to projects and
    support project team cooperation.
  </Lede>

  <BodyText>
    Sponsors upload proposals for their projects. Students review those proposals
    and submit resumes and cover letters to apply. Instructors then go through all
    the documents and decide who should work on which project — weighing equity
    along the way — which is very time-consuming. The site set out to make that
    matching process easier to run.
  </BodyText>
