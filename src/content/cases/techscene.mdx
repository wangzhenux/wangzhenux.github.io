<section id="s01">
  <Lede>
    TechScene is an online event site dedicated to providing event listings
    specific to technology and the tech industry of Austin, Texas.
  </Lede>

  <BodyText>
    Other Austin event sites were broadly focused. The creators of TechScene grew
    tired of wading through multiple meetup and event sites to find centrally
    focused listings of events, classes, and networking get-togethers they wanted
    to attend. The idea: a single manageable place listing happenings and
    opportunities to connect, network, learn, collaborate, play, and socialize —
    without the extraneous listings that did not serve students, enthusiasts, and
    professionals.
  </BodyText>
</section>

<section id="s02">
  <SectionH>User research</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    We interviewed six people aged 22 to 35 in a first phase, then ran a
    competitive analysis of six sites identified by interviewees — including
    Meetup, Eventbrite, Do512, SXSW, and Technical.ly — assessing their design,
    search, and event categorization. A second interview phase with four
    participants balanced the gender mix, and a 13-question Qualtrics survey
    (shared through the iSchool's Insider and the team's tech-industry contacts)
    gathered demographics, event experience, and desired content.
  </BodyText>

<section id="s03">
  <SectionH>Iterative design</SectionH>
  <BodyText>
    From an affinity diagram we identified the site's areas, then moved through
    personas, storyboards, and wireframes in Balsamiq and Axure. We iterated five
    rounds from paper to high fidelity, running usability testing on each — 15
    participants across 25 sessions in total — and refined the pages that most
    affected the user's experience.
  </BodyText>
