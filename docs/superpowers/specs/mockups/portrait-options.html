A · My recommendation

Calligraphic seal — "Z" as a brushstroke

A traditional Chinese chop / yìnzhāng (印章) reimagined: the letter Z rendered as a single decisive sumi-e brushstroke, in clay-terracotta on cream. Authentic to your hobby, dodges the AI-portrait uncanny valley entirely, and reads as a confident designer's mark — the way Pasquale D'Silva or Frank Chimero use a glyph instead of a face.

Sample prompt Calligraphic Chinese seal mark featuring a stylized letter "Z" rendered as a single decisive sumi-e ink brushstroke with natural width variation, rich terracotta clay color (#C8442E) on warm cream paper background (#FAF5E8). Square 1:1 composition, traditional Chinese chop / yìnzhāng aesthetic, minimal, hand-painted feel, no digital artifacts. Subtle paper texture. Editorial illustration.
Generate 3–4 variants in GPT-image-2; pick the one with the most natural brush variation.
B · Strong runner-up

Sumi-e ink-wash portrait

Traditional Chinese ink-wash (水墨画 / sumi-e) style: a few decisive brushstrokes suggest a contemplative figure rather than render one literally. Direct nod to your calligraphy and painting practice. Risk: AI image tools love over-detail — prompts must explicitly fight for sparseness.

Sample prompt Portrait of a man in traditional Chinese sumi-e ink wash painting style, only six or seven minimal brushstrokes total, contemplative quiet expression, looking slightly off-camera, warm cream paper background (#FAF5E8), ink in brown-black with ONE small clay-terracotta accent stroke (#C8442E), square 1:1 composition. Hand-brushed feel — wet edges, ink bleed, paper texture visible. Restrained, scholar-painter aesthetic. NOT photorealistic, NOT detailed, minimal.
Generate 8–10 variants; AI tends to over-render — pick the sparsest.
C · Editorial alternative

Single-line editorial drawing

Tom Gauld / Jean Jullien / New Yorker zone: a continuous-line caricature of you, witty and modern. Less culturally specific than B, more universally "editorial illustrator." Pairs naturally with the warm-paper aesthetic.

Sample prompt Editorial portrait illustration in the style of Tom Gauld or Jean Jullien — single continuous line drawing of a contemplative man, minimal contour, 2-pixel ink line on warm cream paper background (#FAF5E8), one small accent of clay-terracotta (#C8442E) — perhaps a brushstroke or hat or detail. Square 1:1, slightly humorous, slightly thoughtful, hand-drawn feel.
Single-line constraint forces AI to simplify; works on 1st or 2nd try usually.
D · I'd skip this

AI-generated realistic portrait

A photorealistic likeness rendered by AI. The honest take: most senior PD reviewers in 2026 can clock AI-generated faces. If you want a portrait that says "this is what I look like," even a real phone snap or candid beats a polished AI render. If you really want this direction, use Midjourney v7+ with reference photos — but the bar is high to avoid the uncanny valley.

If you want it anyway Use Midjourney v7 / Flux with --cref [reference photo URL]. Style: editorial photography, natural light, warm tones, soft focus on background. NOT studio lit, NOT plastic-skin, NOT cinematic. Slight grain. Square 1:1.
Strong recommendation: use a real photo or skip the portrait entirely.

My recommendation: A · Calligraphic seal

Authentic (you actually do calligraphy), confident (a chop/seal carries weight without taking itself too seriously), distinctive (no other PD portfolio I found uses this), and crucially — free of the AI-portrait awkwardness. The mark can scale: same seal in the About hero, smaller in the nav, micro version as a favicon.

If you want a figure rather than a glyph, B · sumi-e ink-wash is the strong runner-up — same craft authenticity, more human warmth.
